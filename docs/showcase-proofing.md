# A little help with the words.

Spelling and grammar checks run locally with **Harper**. Your words stay on your computer.

## A second pair of eyes

Keep the thought. Let spelling and grammar checks help with the final polish.

Choose a marked word to see suggestions. Add your own names to the dictionary,
choose an English dialect, or bring your team's vocabulary in a Weirpack.

## Room for technical detail

```typescript
const note = {
  title: "A clearer way to write",
  format: "Markdown",
  proofing: "local",
};
```

The equation stays readable too: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

## Your writing, your view

Choose **Orca**, **Classic**, **Frame** or **Nord** in Options. Use Shortcuts for
the keyboard reference, Find to search, and the outline to jump between headings.
